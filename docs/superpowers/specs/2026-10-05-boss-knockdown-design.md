# Boss knockdown after part breaks

## Intent

Part targeting should create a tangible combat opening. When the hunter breaks both targetable body parts of a large creature, it falls over once for a clearly visible attack window.

## Rules

| Creature | Trigger | Knockdown time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cinderback | Vent and tail broken | 1.7 seconds |
| Thornhart | Antlers and hoof broken | 1.7 seconds |
| Ashbell Ram | Horn and resonance chamber broken | 1.8 seconds |

The lowered sprite communicates the state. A heavy strike on an open wound still causes the existing short recover stagger, but cannot replace the longer knockdown earned by a second break.
