# Twin Fangs Combat Stick

## Purpose

Twin Fangs is the third vertical-slice weapon converted from four attack buttons to a floating right-side combat stick. It is built around rapid pressure, positional movement, and Tempo routing.

## Input

- Tap: Cut chain.
- Pull up and release: Rushing Cross.
- Pull down and release: Dodge.
- Hold then release: Retreating Fan.
- Hold then pull up: Overdrive Flurry when Tempo is full.

Drink and Jump remain separate support buttons. The touch surface excludes them, while the part selector sits above the combat region.

## Role

Hits generate Tempo quickly and benefit more from elemental and status tuning than heavy weapons. The player sustains short attack chains, chooses when to retreat with Fan, then spends a full Tempo meter for a committed Overdrive burst. The held upward gesture makes this expenditure deliberate instead of adding another permanent button.

## Verification

Gesture tests cover every command and support-button exclusion. HUD tests verify button removal and bilingual guidance. The live flow test verifies that an Overdrive command spends its 60 Tempo requirement.
