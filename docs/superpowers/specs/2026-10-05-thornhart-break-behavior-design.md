# Thornhart break behavior

## Intent

Thornhart changes its behavior when its targetable body parts break. The hunter can remove a dangerous ranged threat, stop the long chase attack, or preserve the parts to face a more varied second phase.

## Moves and breaks

| State | Available moves | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intact, phase one | Charge, Stomp, Thorns | Full baseline rhythm |
| Intact, phase two | Charge, Stomp, Thorns, Briar Lash | A charge can continue into a close thorn lash |
| Antlers broken | Charge, Stomp | Thorns and Briar Lash are unavailable |
| Hoof broken | Stomp, Thorns, Briar Lash in phase two | Charge is unavailable |
| Both broken | Stomp | A predictable finishing phase |

## Recovery window

After every fourth completed attack sequence, Thornhart becomes exhausted for 1.3 seconds. Its sprite lowers in that state to communicate the opening.
