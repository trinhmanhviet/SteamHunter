# Beast Materials and Coats

The hunt loop gains creature-specific trophies and protective coats. Cinderback drops ash plates; Thornhart drops thorn antlers. Breaking the monster's armored part yields an extra trophy. Both the existing iron parts and trophies persist in the save file. Results show the rewards by name, so the player can choose the hunt needed for a desired coat.

The gear screen gains a coat tab alongside weapons. The starting field coat is always owned. An ember coat costs four iron parts and two ash plates; it adds health and softens hits. A thorn vest costs four iron parts and two thorn antlers; it adds stamina and makes dodging cheaper. Buying a coat equips it, and owned coats can be changed without spending more. Coat names are plain English or everyday Vietnamese, with original pixel-art item illustrations.

The hunter receives the equipped coat on hunt start. Combat calculations use one rules function for damage reduction and one for dodge cost; the HUD scales to the coat's maximum health and stamina. Old saves migrate to an empty trophy stock and the field coat. Tests cover rewards, save migration, buying and equipping, health/stamina changes, and result display. The Android build is installed and inspected on the connected phone.
